Publications

Research papers and preprints.

GUARD: A Prompt-Level Discipline for Correcting Categorical Gist Substitution in Large Language Model Characterizations of Institutions

SSRN Preprint · 2026

Valerie Riggs, Emmanuel Masa-Ibi, Daniel Onyejiekwe, Yosmehily Segura-Silverio, Ato Bhatta · Morgan State University

Introduces GUARD (Ground, Unbundle, Anchor, Resolve, Distill), a prompt-level intervention grounded in fuzzy-trace theory that corrects how LLMs characterize institutions such as HBCUs. Paired with a deterministic checker, GUARD reduced failure-signature prevalence from 51.0% to 0.0% and raised mean gist displacement from 0.79 to 2.39 (Cohen's d = 1.70) on a 17-prompt HBCU benchmark.
